01 / Our project

Making the most of small green spaces

Our project focuses on simple improvements that students can make around the school. We are adding and maintaining small patches of grass in suitable areas instead of making major changes.

These little garden spaces can make the school look more cared for and remind everyone that nature deserves attention—even in small places.

  • ✓Plant grass in suitable empty spaces.
  • ✓Keep garden beds tidy and cared for.
  • ✓Encourage students to protect the greenery.

03 / Organised volunteering

Volunteering works better together

Organised volunteering is planned by a school, foundation, charity, or community group. It gives volunteers clear tasks and helps activities create a lasting benefit.

🌿

Environmental volunteering

Join a school gardening day, plant suitable greenery, or help maintain shared outdoor spaces. These activities make the campus more welcoming and encourage environmental responsibility.

📚

Educational volunteering

Help younger students with reading, homework, or basic digital skills through a school-organised programme. Sharing knowledge can build confidence and support learning.

🤲

Social and charity volunteering

Take part in a donation drive or community visit organised by a trusted group. These efforts can provide practical support and help people feel included.

04 / Local volunteering

Positive actions in our community

There are many ways to volunteer close to home in Indonesia. Local activities help improve everyday life and strengthen relationships between neighbours.

🧹

Neighbourhood clean-up

Collect litter in approved public areas and sort waste where facilities are available. Cleaner surroundings are safer and more pleasant for everyone.

🌳

Tree and plant care

Plant suitable trees or maintain community gardens with permission. Green spaces provide shade, support local nature, and beautify the area.

🥫

Food and clothing donations

Help collect and organise useful donations through a trusted school or community programme. This can support families facing difficult circumstances.

🧑‍🏫

Teaching and tutoring

Assist children with reading or schoolwork in a supervised programme. Sharing time and knowledge can help learners feel supported.

05 / International volunteering ideas

Small efforts can have a global impact

International volunteering can connect people across countries to address shared challenges. Students can learn about these efforts and support suitable, trusted initiatives.

🐢

Environmental conservation

Support reputable conservation campaigns that protect forests, oceans, and wildlife. Cooperation helps preserve natural resources for future generations.

🆘

Disaster relief support

Contribute through established humanitarian organisations collecting approved supplies or donations. Coordinated support can help affected communities recover.

🌏

International education projects

Join supervised cultural or learning exchanges that share educational resources and ideas. These programmes build understanding and cooperation between communities.
